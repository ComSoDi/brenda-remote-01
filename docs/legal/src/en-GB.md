# About aiBrenda

_Last updated: October 2026_

# What is aiBrenda?

aiBrenda is an app for having a chat. Brenda is a conversation companion designed especially for older people. You can talk to her about whatever you like, whenever you like: your day, your memories, your hobbies or whatever comes up.

Brenda is not a real person. She was developed with artificial intelligence, so she has all the time in the world, a wide general knowledge and endless patience.

# How does it work?

- **TALK mode:** tap the button and talk to Brenda as if you were on a phone call.
- **WRITE mode:** if you prefer, write to her and she will write back.
- **Brenda remembers you:** she keeps a few details from your chats, as text, so that your conversations feel more natural.
- **Brenda forgets whenever you want:** if you want Brenda to forget something, just tell her: “Brenda, forget that I like chocolate cake”.

# How much does it cost?

When you install aiBrenda from Google Play you get a **free account** with a certain amount of conversation time every month. If you want more time, you can buy a **top-up** or choose the **subscription** plan that suits you best (Basic, Superior or Advanced). All payments are made securely through Google Play. More details in our [Terms and Conditions](terms.html).

# Brenda is very discreet

Brenda is not a social network like Facebook, Twitter or WhatsApp, where everyone finds out what you say. Brenda does not share your conversations with other Brenda users.

# Your privacy

- We do not record or keep your voice.
- We do not ask for your address or your bank details.
- We do not ask for health, financial or legal information.
- We do not sell your data or show you adverts.

You can find more details in our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

# What Brenda is not

Brenda is not a person, and she sometimes gets things wrong. She is not your doctor, your lawyer or your adviser: for any question on those subjects she will recommend that you speak to a professional.

!! Brenda is not an emergency service either: **if you need urgent help, call 999 or 112, or the emergency number in your country.**

# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

# 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that some websites store in your browser to remember things, such as the fact that you have already signed in. There are similar technologies, such as the browser’s “local storage”, that work in a similar way. In this policy, when we say “cookies” we mean all of them.

# 2. What we use on aiBrenda

**aiBrenda does not use advertising cookies, social media cookies or third-party analytics cookies** (such as Google Analytics). We do not follow you around other websites or build profiles to show you adverts.

We only use, when needed, **strictly necessary technical elements** so that the website works. For example:

- Keeping you signed in while you use aiBrenda.
- Remembering basic settings, such as whether you prefer to talk or write.
- Protecting the website against attacks and automated access. When we use Cloudflare, our network and security provider, it may set some technical cookies for this purpose.

| Name | What it is for | Duration |
| `brenda_session` | Keeps you signed in. | 30 days |
| Local storage | Remembers basic app settings on your device. | Until you clear it |
| `__cf_bm` | Cloudflare: tells people apart from bots. Only when Cloudflare protects the website. | 30 minutes |
| `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare: remembers that you have passed a security check. Only when Cloudflare protects the website. | Usually 30 minutes (1 year at most) |

Under Spanish law, which applies to us (Article 22.2 of the LSSI, the Spanish Information Society Services Act), these technical cookies do not need your consent, because they are essential for our service to work. That is why we do not show you a notice asking you to accept them.

## Technical monitoring (New Relic)

We use New Relic to detect faults and check that the website is running quickly. If any data is stored in your browser for this purpose, we will list it above.

# 3. What about usage statistics?

Yes, we analyse how aiBrenda is used in order to improve it (for example, which countries visitors come from, which pages they view, how long each visit lasts, how many minutes of conversation are used or which features fail). To do this we use the activity data from our own servers and Umami, a statistics tool that **uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser** and does not identify you personally. You can find more information in our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

# 4. In the Android app

The aiBrenda app does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Please bear in mind that Google Play and your phone have their own policies, which do not depend on us.

# 5. How to delete or block cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge…), usually under “Privacy” or “Security”. If you block technical cookies, you may have to sign in every time, or some features may not work properly.

# 6. Changes

If in the future we add cookies that are not technical (for example, analytics or advertising cookies), we will explain it here and **ask for your permission first** before setting them.

# 7. Contact

To protect your privacy, we recommend that you do not use your real name when you create an account, and certainly not your full name.

When you use TALK mode, the microphone picks up your voice and sends it in real time to the artificial intelligence provider (currently Google Gemini) so that Brenda can understand you and reply. **aiBrenda never records, stores or keeps the audio.** Nor do we keep any biometric data from your voice (what is known as a “voiceprint”).

What you and Brenda say is converted into text (“transcribed”). From those transcripts we keep:

Information about how the service is used: when you connect, how long you talk, how many Brendys (tokens) you use, technical errors and the type of device. This information is essential for the system to work, for keeping track of your Brendy (token) balance and for improving aiBrenda.

## What Brendys, or tokens, are

Tokens (which we call Brendys) are a unit of measurement used in computing, especially in artificial intelligence. A very common word (e.g. “December”) may count as a single token, while a rarely used one (e.g. “antonomasia”) would probably need five tokens (one per syllable). We cannot predict exactly how many tokens are used per minute of conversation; we only have an estimate. That is why aiBrenda’s services are used and paid for in tokens, not in conversation time.

## Your purchase data

Google Play tells us which plan or top-up you have bought and whether it is still active. **We do not receive your payment details.**
