2. What you are buying

With aiBrenda you buy conversation time with Brenda, an artificial intelligence companion, by voice or in writing. It is a digital entertainment service. It does not include physical products or home delivery, so we do not need your postal address.

3. How we measure and charge for the service: Introducing "tokens"

  • Tokens are the units artificial intelligence providers use to measure the work they do. Every time you talk or write and Brenda replies, tokens are used.
  • We call tokens Brendys. One Brendy equals one token.
  • There are voice tokens (Brendys) (TALK mode) and chat tokens (Brendys) (WRITE mode). Talking usually uses more tokens than writing.
  • To make things easier, we also show your Brendys as an approximate amount of usage time. This equivalence is calculated using the formulas set by our artificial intelligence providers (for example, Google Gemini, OpenAI ChatGPT or ElevenLabs).
  • Please note that the time figure is only an approximation. What you actually spend depends on how long you talk, how long the replies are, the pauses, whether you talk more or write more, and other factors we cannot control).
  • Providers may change their calculations and prices at any time. If that happens, we may have to our prices, and we will show this in the app. But, don't worry: any Brendys you already have will not lose value: you keep the same amount until you use them.

4. Available plans

  • Free account: when you install the app from Google Play you get a free account, with no expiry date, with a limited amount of voice Brendys and a limited amount of chat Brendys that you can use each month free of charge. If we ever change the number of free Brendys we give the free account, or ever withdraw this option, we will let you know in advance.
  • Top-up: a one-time payment that lets you add more Brendys to your account without signing up for a subscription. You can top up whenever you like, but be careful! Only do it when you really need more time.
  • Top-up Brendys you have not used are carried over from month to month. They only expire after 24 months without use. For example, if you bought 50 top-up Brendys on the 26th of the month and your subscription plan renews with 200 Brendys, you will have 250 Brendys at the start of the next period.
  • Basic, Superior and Advanced subscriptions: three recurring paid plans. Each one includes more Brendys than the one before, at a lower price per Brendy. You can see the current prices by tapping “My info” and then “More Time”.
  • Before you buy, the app and Google Play will show you the final price (including taxes), the Brendys included and, for subscriptions, how often they renew.
  • Important note: subscription Brendys renew every month, on the same date you subscribed, but they are not added to any you did not use in the previous period. For example, if you had 50 Brendys left on your 200-Brendy monthly subscription plan, you will have 200 Brendys when the new period begins.
