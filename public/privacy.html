Privacy Policy

The IABrenda.com app is currently in a trial period and is only available to select users collaborating in the preliminary testing of this development stage.

During this trial period we cannot guarantee the privacy of data used to discover potential bugs, unforeseen requirements, and features to be added before its future launch.

We are also not responsible for the privacy of data belonging to anyone who uses IABrenda.com without our consent.

The registration system used during the trial period (Nickname + PIN) is VERY basic and does not remotely guarantee data privacy.

Please NEVER SHARE personal data such as your address, emails, bank account, health status, legal or tax matters, etc. on this platform.

Once the trial period ends, we will implement more robust protection systems (e.g. OAuth).

Voice & Conversation Data

Voice input: When you use TALK mode, your microphone captures your voice and sends it in real time to our AI processing service. Audio is processed immediately and is never recorded, stored, or retained by IA Brenda or its servers.

Transcriptions: Voice conversations are transcribed to text in real time. These transcriptions, along with your text chat messages, are saved to display your approximately last 20 interactions. This allows you to review recent conversations within the app. Conversation history is associated exclusively with your account and is stored securely.

What we do not store: Raw audio files, voice recordings, or any biometric voice data.

Data deletion: You can request deletion of your account and all associated conversation data at any time by contacting us at support@comerciosocialdigital.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

Política de Privacidad de Datos

Datos de Voz y Conversación

Entrada de voz: Cuando usas el modo HABLAR, tu micrófono capta tu voz y la envía en tiempo real a nuestro servicio de procesamiento de IA. El audio se procesa al instante y nunca es grabado, almacenado ni conservado por IA Brenda ni por sus servidores.

Transcripciones: Las conversaciones de voz se transcriben a texto en tiempo real. Estas transcripciones, junto con tus mensajes de chat de texto, se guardan para mostrar tus aproximadamente últimas 20 interacciones. Esto te permite revisar tus conversaciones recientes dentro de la app. El historial de conversación está asociado exclusivamente a tu cuenta y se almacena de forma segura.

Lo que no almacenamos: Archivos de audio en bruto, grabaciones de voz ni ningún dato biométrico de voz.

Eliminación de datos: Puedes solicitar la eliminación de tu cuenta y de todos los datos de conversación asociados en cualquier momento escribiéndonos a support@comerciosocialdigital.com. Procesaremos tu solicitud en un plazo de 30 días.
